Hextoks · bilingual heritage pair · concept prototype · version 3

Sandstone Hextoks حَجَر

Two site hextoks rest on plinths with a tag reader between them. Each token has three concept sides: STONE · حَجَر, MARKS · نَقش, DWELLING · مَسكَن. Rotate the tokens so the same concept faces the reader to compare how Hegra and Mesa Verde express it. Tap the top of a token to explore the site.

At the table

Two tables, eight activities

In an exhibit, this website runs on tablets beside two tables of objects. Activities 1–4 happen at the game table and 5–8 at the heritage desk. Each activity below has a short clip showing what hands do at the table.

Where in the world?

Read each pin’s coordinates. Tap a pin, then tap its place on the map (or drag it). The grid lines help. When a pin lands in the right place, the map zooms in on that country. When both pins are in, drag from one pin to the other to stretch the string.

Coordinates: UNESCO, Hegra · UNESCO, Mesa Verde. Map outlines: Natural Earth
