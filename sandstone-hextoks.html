Token skins

Each hextok unfolded: the top face and its six side panels, in order around the token. Concept sides alternate with the site's name and inscription year. Print, cut, and wrap around a 5 cm cardboard or popsicle-stick token; each concept side hides an NFC tag.

Sensing idea: a cheap NFC tag sits inside each concept side, and one reader between the plinths reads only the two sides facing it, so no magnetometer is needed.
